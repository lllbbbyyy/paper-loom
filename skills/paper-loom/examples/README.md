This is a fictional **proposal**, not a completed empirical paper. It demonstrates how a short abstract, introduction, method sketch, evaluation plan, and conclusion connect to independent section, paragraph, and sentence records. Every prose sentence has its own file. The two language versions share IDs here for easy comparison, but a real translation may need one-to-many mappings.

这是虚构的**方案示例**，不是已完成的实证论文。它展示摘要、引言、方法概要、评价计划和结论如何连接独立的章节、段落与句子记录。每句正文都有自己的文件。这里为便于对照使用相同 ID；真实翻译可以采用一对多映射。

Follow the links from the argument map down to the sentence cards. `index.json` records reading order and supports a structural consistency check. The prose is the wording authority. The checker verifies coverage and links, not the scientific merit or fluency of the argument.

从全文提纲逐级点击即可查看句子卡。`index.json` 保存阅读顺序，供结构一致性检查使用。正文是措辞准本。检查器核对覆盖与链接，不判定科学价值或语言流畅性。
